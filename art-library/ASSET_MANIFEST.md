**Pending** means the asset is present in the repository but author/licensing metadata has not yet been verified in this catalogue. It does not imply the asset is unlicensed.

Third-party ArtStation images are intentionally excluded from this manifest because they have not been licensed or uploaded as game-ready assets.

When adding assets, record source, author, creation method, licence, tile footprint, collision footprint, animation frames and in-game status.
