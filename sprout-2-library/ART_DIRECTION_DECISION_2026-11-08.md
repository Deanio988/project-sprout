# Project Sprout 2.0 — Art direction decision

**User selected Option A: Cute Fantasy Free as the primary visual direction.**

## Scope of decision
- Aim for a cosy, colourful woodland and cottage aesthetic.
- Keep Sunnyside, Mystic Woods, Pixel 16 Woods and Tiny Swords as separate archived alternatives. Do not mix packs without explicit user approval.
- This selects a **visual direction**, not an approved sprite-by-sprite manifest.
- **No map or gameplay code is authorised by this decision.**
- Resolve exact source asset selection, interior coverage, pond shoreline and joins before map construction.

## Licensing gate
The included Cute Fantasy Free licence states **non-commercial use only** and prohibits redistribution of the assets, even modified. Do not commit raw sprites, derivatives or contact sheets to a public repository or deploy publicly accessible asset bundles under the current terms. If commercial use or public redistribution is intended, obtain appropriate permissions/licence from the rights holder before proceeding.
