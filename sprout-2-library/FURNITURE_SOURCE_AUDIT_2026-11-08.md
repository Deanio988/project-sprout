# Furniture source audit — 2026-11-08

Inspected actual source PNGs in user-supplied `back ups.zip`, nested `mystic_woods_free_2.2.zip` and `BETA Sunnyside World - Tileset_GameMaker.zip`. Private review bundle `Project_Sprout_2_Furniture_Audit.zip` contains original-source contact sheet, inventory CSV, and findings; no sprite imagery committed to public repo.

## Confirmed candidates
- Mystic Woods 2.2 `sprites/objects/chest_01.png` and `chest_02.png`: 64×16 sprite strips, storage chest frames.
- Sunnyside Beta `spr_deco_chair_01` (10×14), `spr_deco_sidetable_01` (12×26), `spr_deco_book_01` (7×8), `spr_deco_book_02` (5×9), `spr_deco_chest_01_closed` (16×21). These are a **third, unapproved** art family.
- Mystic Woods `sprites/objects/objects.png` includes multiple prominently **Premium Version!** marked objects; they must not be counted as free usable furniture.
- Mystic Woods decor_16x16 and decor_8x8 are mainly terrain decorations, not an interior furnishing set.

## Conclusion
No verified bed, sofa, fireplace, kitchen or complete furnished cottage set among these inspected files. Mystic Woods flooring, carpet, walls and doors remain viable **candidate** room-shell art. Sunnyside furniture may help only if user authorizes a separate visual trial; mixing is not yet approved. Keep Cute Fantasy exterior approved, Mystic Woods interior candidate-only, Sunnyside furniture unapproved. No map/gameplay built.

## Rights
Free packs have restrictive non-commercial/no-redistribution licence conditions. No raw art or derived contact sheets on public GitHub; obtain appropriate rights before any public asset deployment.
